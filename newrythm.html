THE PROBLEM

Stress conditions need to be predicted accurately from physiological measures such as heart rate and related health signals.

SOLUTION

Use machine learning models on engineered health data to identify stress states. A model comparison shows that decision trees and KNN are the best models in terms of accuracy for this task.

SCOPE

The project covers:
- Data engineering and preprocessing
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Model training and evaluation (KNN vs. Decision Trees)

TARGET USERS

Who are we trying to target and why?

  • Healthcare Professionals
  • Data Scientists
  • Wellness App Developers
